The '''MS-06R Psycho Zaku Mk-II''' is a custom [[mobile suit]] from the ''[[Mobile Suit Gundam Thunderbolt (Manga)|Mobile Suit Gundam Thunderbolt]]'' manga.

==Technology & Combat Characteristics==
The Psycho Zaku Mk-II is a custom mobile suit based on the [[Principality of Zeon]]'s mainstay mobile suit, the [[MS-06 Zaku II (Thunderbolt Ver.)|MS-06 Zaku II]]. It is equipped with the Reuse "P" Device, just like its predecessor [[MS-06R Zaku II High Mobility Type (Reuse "P" Device)]]. This allows the pilot to control the mobile suit as if it were their own limbs, as a result of the specialized prosthetic limbs that the pilot wears. The Mk-II has considerably lighter armor as compared to the original Psycho Zaku, which combined with the Reuse "P" Device, makes it an overly fast and agile mobile suit, even compared to the original. The Psycho Zaku Mk-II unit piloted by Daryl Lorenz was intended to be equipped with additional armaments and enhancements to allow it to be used in space, but it never received this equipment. The other Psycho Zaku Mk-II units piloted by members of the [[South Seas Alliance]] in space are equipped and armed in much the same way as the original Psycho Zaku of the Thunderbolt Sector, such as the large rocket booster. 

==Armaments==
;*Shoulder Shield
:Mounted on the right shoulder, it is a simple defense used to block most incoming ballistic and explosive projectiles.
;*Heat Hawk
:An axe-shaped close combat weapon, it uses a super-heated blade to cut through the armor of enemy MS or vehicles. The thermal energy is generated by the power output of the Psycho Zaku MK-II's thermonuclear reactor and relayed through the MS' hands. The Psycho Zaku Mk-II unit used by Daryl Lorenz is armed with two such weapons, used in a pair.

;*880mmRB-T27 Raketen Bazooka
:A new heavy bazooka which features powerful rocket-propelled rounds, and improved targeting sensor with protective cover for aiming. It is fed by a 5 round magazine.

;*MMP-78 120mm Machine Gun
:A standard firearm for Zaku type MS. It has a large drum magazine located at the top, and features a scope and foregrip to enable highly accurate shooting. It can switch modes between single-fire and automatic fire. Although useless against Luna Titanium Alloy, it can still destroy weaker armor material with multiple shots. This one is equipped with an under-barrel grenade launcher.

;*120mm Machine Gun
:The 120mm machine gun is a standard weapon for Zaku type mobile suits. It is a shell firing gun that does not require energy to be used, however its effectiveness against heavy armor is very limited. Two of these weapons are stored under the lower propellant tank. While four spare drums are stored on the top rocket booster.

;*360mm Giant Bazooka
:A large bazooka originally designed for use by the Dom series of mobile suits. The 360mm bazooka is magazine fed and holds 10 rounds per magazine, and a single round is powerful enough to blow the torso off of an enemy mobile suit. Three of these are stored under the top rocket booster.

;*280mm Zaku Bazooka
:The 280mm Zaku Bazooka was another standard weapon that could be equipped to a Zaku type mobile suit. The bazooka fired explosive rounds that were quite effective against the armor of Earth Federation mobile suits and battleships. The Psycho Zaku Mk-II carries one bazooka handheld and 4 spare magazines hooked to the top propellant tank.

;*MIP-B6 Cracker Grenade
:Identical to those used by infantry, the cracker grenade is an explosive weapon designed to be thrown or launched by the MS and detonate after a set amount of time. The "cracker" breaks into several smaller munitions over a wide area. The Zaku II can optionally equip up to four cracker grenades, that are stored on the left and right waist armor racks, with two on each side.

==Special Equipment & Features==
;*Reuse P (Psycho) Device
:A next generation operating system that has the potential to operate a mobile suit like a pilot's arms and legs by communicating to the mobile suit's drive system directly via an electric signal transmitted from the pilot's brain. Even individuals with little piloting experience can master the controls of the Reuse P Device in a short period because on a base level, the pilot only need to thinks about movement.

;* Sub Arm
:Like the [[MS-06 Zaku II (Thunderbolt Ver.)|MS-06 Zaku II]] and the original Psycho Zaku, two sub arms are mounted on the backpack, as it mounts a large arsenal of handheld weapons and spare magazines on its boosters' weapon racks.

;*Rocket Booster
:The Psycho Zaku Mk-II can attach a maximum of one large rocket booster and two smaller boosters on its backpack to increase its thruster output, realizing high output and high mobility. They also serve as propellant tanks, with the smaller boosters also functioning as weapon racks. Can be ejected from the backpack when emptied.

==History==
At the end of the One Year War, the [[Newtype]] monk [[Levan Fuu]] secretly acquires the remains of the Psycho Zaku and transports it to his breakaway [[South Seas Alliance]] on Earth. His brainwashed forces begin repairing the Psycho Zaku and continuing research into the Reuse "P" Device under the supervision of former Zeon scientist [[J.J. Sexton]], who was responsible for its creation alongside [[Karla Mitchum]] in the Thunderbolt Sector. After the war, both the [[Zeon Remnants]] led by General Kleiber and the ''Pegasus''-class ''Spartan'' of the [[Earth Federation Forces]] breach into Alliance territory in order to recover the Reuse "P" Device technology.

[[Daryl Lorenz]], who had been sent by the Zeon Remnants to recover the Psycho Zaku, eventually encounters Commander [[Claudia Peer]] of the Alliance, whom influences his decision to defect from Zeon and take Karla with him to the Alliance's Taal Volcano base in the Philippines. There, he witnesses 32 Psycho Zaku Mk-IIs under maintenance and Levan Fuu, who reveals to Daryl his plan for the units to strike against [[Anaheim Electronics]] on the [[Moon]] by seizing the [[Solar Ray]]. Fuu also heals Karla's fractured mind so that she may help finalize work on the Mk-II. Daryl begins training with an unarmored prototype synched to his pilot data, Unit 1, which he later uses to man a [[Big Gun]] to attack the approaching ''Spartan''. Once the base is breached by the Federation mobile suits, Daryl narrowly escapes a fatal attack from [[Io Fleming]] in the [[RX-78AL Atlas Gundam]] that destroys Unit 1. Daryl switches to the fully-armored Unit 2, which he uses to single-handedly sink the ''Spartan'' with only two Heat Hawks and thus allow the other 32 Psycho Zaku Mk-II units to successfully escape into space.

In order to retrofit his ground-use Unit 2 for space combat, Daryl and Zeon Remnant defectors head to a hidden space fortress containing the remains of the [[FA-78 Full Armor Gundam (Thunderbolt Ver.)|FA-78 Full Armor Gundam]] recovered by General Kleiber's forces in the Thunderbolt Sector. Daryl's unit is converted into the [[Perfect Gundam (Thunderbolt Ver.)|Perfect Gundam]], which he uses to infiltrate [[Luna II]] and steal the [[MAN-03 Braw Bro (Thunderbolt)|MAN-03 Braw Bro]].

The other Psycho Zaku Mk-IIs are recovered by the Alliance once they escape the [[Earth]]. Three units undergo training at a facility located within a derelict colony in Side 4 before they are attacked by the [[MSN-02 Perfect Zeong (Thunderbolt Ver.)|Perfect Zeong]] piloted by Io Fleming and other mobile suits of the ''Beehive II'', and are ultimately destroyed. The remaining 29 Psycho Zaku Mk-IIs and other Alliances forces join up to commence the operation to capture the Side 3 colonies and the Solar Ray, with [[Solomon|Konpei Island]] and the Earth Federation Forces in their way. The Psycho Zaku team consisting of [[Fisher Ness]], Boris, and Matthew sortied as the vanguard and single-handedly broke through Konpei Island's defensive line with just three units. The remaining 26 Psycho Zaku units advanced to capture the Solar Ray, even managing to repel the Trust Squad that had arrived as reinforcements.

The head of the Psycho Zaku Mk-II was equipped with a system that allowed Karla, aboard the [[MA-08_Big_Zam_(Thunderbolt_Ver.)|Big Zam]], to remotely transmit a high-voltage electric current through it, electrocuting the pilot and causing fatal brain damage. Afterward, control of the unit could be switched back to remote operation. The only individuals unharmed by this were Fisher, Boris, and Matthew, who were away from their Psycho Zaku Mk-IIs for maintenance and resupply when the incident occurred (though their machines were hijacked), and Daryl, whose unit’s original head had been removed during its conversion into the Perfect Gundam.

The remotely controlled Psycho Zaku Mk-IIs headed toward seven evacuation colonies housing 150 million Zeon civilians, intending to massacre them. However, when Karla lost control during the Solar Ray’s firing sequence, Levan Fuu used the last of his strength to disable all 26 Psycho Zakus, which were then immediately destroyed by a concentrated volley from the Earth Federation garrison stationed in Zeon.

After the war three units were recovered by Philip Kaufman and will attempt to continue Levan legacy
